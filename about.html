Our Story

Born from passion. Built for fragrance lovers.

Scentifymee exists because blind-buying a ₹15,000 bottle of perfume felt like gambling — and the decant market at the time was either untrustworthy or unaffordable.

How It Started

I have always been captivated by fragrance — the way a single spritz can teleport you to a place, a memory, a feeling. What started as casual curiosity slowly became an obsession with niche houses, vintage finds, and everything in between.

But collecting fragrances is expensive. I made the mistake of blind-buying bottles I ended up never wearing. That is when I discovered the world of decants — smaller samples that let you experience a fragrance properly before investing in a full bottle.

The problem? The decant market in India was messy. Half the sellers could not prove authenticity, and the ones that could charged almost as much as the bottle itself. I kept thinking: there has to be a better way.

So I started Scentifymee — filling decants from bottles I personally own, pricing them fairly, and being completely transparent about what is inside each vial. What began as a passion project is now a community of fragrance lovers who believe you should smell extraordinary without emptying your wallet.

— Founder, Scentifymee
